Who We Are

A Contractor Built on Reliability

Al Rujuh General Contracting Est. is one of the Western Region's most respected service providers. Since 2005 we have grown from a local Yanbu contractor into a full-service establishment trusted by national and international EPC companies alike.

We are genuinely a different sort of contracting firm. Our job is to give every client the finest workmanship possible — and that begins with a commitment and a caring attitude toward the work itself.

  • Quality, detail and safety — non-negotiableSince inception these three have set us apart from every other general contractor.
