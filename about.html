Our Story

Definitely a Different Sort of Contracting Firm

Operating since 2005, Al Rujuh General Contracting Est. has built a reputation across the Kingdom that few contractors of our size can match. What began as a Yanbu-based general contractor has grown into a full-service establishment supporting industrial, commercial and residential clients throughout the region.

We are currently taking care of small and medium-size construction across civil, mechanical and electrical disciplines — alongside supply and trading for construction requirements, accommodation with catering to industrial standards, and full manpower deployment.

Manpower is the very important strength behind all successful projects. At Al Rujuh we combine the latest technology with genuinely experienced, creatively driven professionals to execute the works properly the first time.

Having completed more than 300 projects, our teams are dedicated, creative and detail oriented. And we keep it that way — because a contractor's reputation is built one handover at a time.
